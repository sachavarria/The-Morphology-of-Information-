# The Morphology of Information

**Edition 01**

An ongoing artistic and computational investigation into the organization of information across mathematical, biological and planetary systems.

By S. A. Chavarría, anti-disciplinary artist, writer, researcher and professor, developed through sustained conversation with artificial intelligence.

The project began with an encounter with a widely circulated visualization of the dependencies in a proposed formal proof of the Riemann hypothesis. It examines the relationship between visual morphology and network topology through comparative computational experiments.

The research is exploratory; scientific novelty and statistical robustness remain subject to independent scrutiny. The interactive atlas and accompanying methodology will be released following review of source attribution and dataset redistribution permissions.
