# The Morphology of Information

**Edition 02 / 2026**

**[Enter the interactive atlas](https://sachavarria.github.io/The-Morphology-of-Information-/)**

An ongoing artistic and computational investigation into the organization of information across mathematical, biological and planetary systems.

S. A. Chavarría · Anti-disciplinary artist, writer, researcher and professor.

Developed through sustained research in conversation with artificial intelligence.

## About the research

The project began with an encounter with a widely circulated visualization of the dependencies in a proposed formal proof of the Riemann hypothesis. It examines the relationship between visual morphology and network topology through comparative computational experiments across formal mathematics, bacterial gene regulation, neuronal connectivity and river drainage systems.

The collaborative methodology emerges from a relationship with artificial intelligence developed over years, across different models, technological architectures and artistic investigations. The inquiry evolves through proposition, debate, rejection, experimentation and revision.

The experimental findings remain exploratory and open to independent verification. Scientific novelty and statistical robustness remain subject to further scrutiny.

## Sources and reproducibility

The atlas documents five experiments, their findings, methodological limitations and dataset provenance. The public edition embeds attributed mathematical network samples and provides local data loading for other empirical networks whose redistribution terms require additional verification. Original third-party datasets are not included in this repository.

The website is a standalone HTML document. Source attribution and research qualifications are included within the atlas.
