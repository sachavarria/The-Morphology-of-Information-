Technical record

The five-vertex, six-edge configuration class is held fixed throughout. The first rule permits two-edge switches, the second adds directed triangle reversals, and the third permits any valid replacement of at most three edges. The 21-state class and transition sets come from the published example; the timings, geometry and order of attempted operations are composed for this study. A proposed swap that introduces reciprocal edges is shown and then withdrawn, because reciprocal degree must remain zero. The final state contains an induced feedforward motif. Reachable-state counts refer to state 01, not to the number of states visited during playback.
