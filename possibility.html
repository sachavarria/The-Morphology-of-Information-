Experimental record / exact reference

All 21 labeled graphs have five vertices, six directed edges and identical node-specific in-, out- and reciprocal degrees. Six contain an induced feedforward motif. Exact uniform reference: 6/21 = 28.57%. Edge switches from state 01 reach six states; adding triangle reversals reaches fifteen. Rejected attempts remain in place. Uniform reference samples every state independently; it is not a local transformation.

Triangle mode mixes the original switch with a uniformly selected triple with probability one half each. This synthetic counterexample does not establish that the actual neural connectome's state space is disconnected. Geometry is illustrative; transitions are drawn from Experiment 007.
